# Architecture Walkthrough — Synthetic Engineering Persona

1. **Evidence:** data-logging and CAD prototypes are useful technical evidence, but production deployment/DFM ownership remain out of bounds.
2. **Role hypotheses:** PRIMARY could be Test Engineer or Integration Engineer; ADJACENT could be Embedded Systems; production manufacturing design may be EXPERIMENTAL.
3. **Artifact choice:** a one-page technical resume may be appropriate for some U.S. roles, but the core system does not force it.
4. **Triage:** distinguish prototype evidence from a JD that requires production ownership.
5. **Domain reviewer:** use an engineering specialist derived from the exact technical responsibilities.
6. **Interview prep:** technical/project deep dives are appropriate here because the target domain calls for them, not because the Career OS assumes every candidate is technical.

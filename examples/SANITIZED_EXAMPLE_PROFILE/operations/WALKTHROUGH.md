# Architecture Walkthrough — Synthetic Operations Persona

1. **Evidence:** scheduling, event handoffs, and vendor coordination are recorded with contribution/ownership boundaries.
2. **Role hypotheses:** PRIMARY might be Operations Coordinator; ADJACENT could be Business Operations or Implementation Coordinator; an Operations Manager family may remain EXPERIMENTAL until people/process ownership is established.
3. **Discovery:** search titles beyond “operations” while keeping function and seniority bounded.
4. **Triage:** an employer asking for workforce planning ownership exposes a real gap even though the scheduling evidence is relevant.
5. **Domain reviewer:** use a senior operations lens rather than a technical-principal lens.
6. **Interview prep:** prioritize process scenarios, stakeholder coordination, and examples of handling operational ambiguity—not coding questions unless the JD actually asks for them.

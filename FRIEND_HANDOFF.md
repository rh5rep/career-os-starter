# Career Search OS — Friend Pilot Handoff

Career Search OS is a private workspace that helps you organize your career facts once, find and evaluate jobs, prepare tailored application materials, track interviews and outcomes, and learn from the search. You mostly use it through normal conversation.

## Create your private copy

1. Open the [Career Search OS starter](https://github.com/rh5rep/career-os-starter).
2. Choose **Use this template → Create a new repository**.
3. Select your own GitHub account, choose a name such as `my-career-search-os`, and set visibility to **Private**.
4. Connect that new private repository to ChatGPT/Codex using the GitHub connection available to you.
5. Open `user_actions/START.md` and send its single onboarding prompt.

Your personal information belongs only in your private copy, never in the public starter. If **Use this template** is unavailable, follow the download/upload fallback in `SETUP_GITHUB.md`.

## Helpful materials to prepare

Bring whatever you already have. You do not need every item:

- resume or CV;
- LinkedIn export or profile notes;
- cover-letter examples;
- portfolio or work samples;
- certifications or licenses;
- transcript or other useful records;
- preferred resume, CV, or cover-letter templates.

Uploaded materials are evidence to reconcile, not automatic truth. You can always say “I don’t know,” “I need to verify,” or “I think so, but I’m not sure.” The system should preserve that uncertainty.

## What onboarding asks

Onboarding proceeds in manageable rounds. It covers contact details, education, work history, responsibilities, accomplishments, metrics, ownership versus contribution, skills, certifications, authorization and sponsorship, location and relocation, work arrangement, compensation constraints, industries and exclusions, work-environment and lifestyle preferences, role ideas, networking preferences, interview readiness, and availability.

Before setup is complete, you receive a plain-English review of your identity/current state, education, employment, strongest accomplishments, skills, certifications, authorization, preferences, role hypotheses, registered templates, and every important unknown or item on hold. Correct anything wrong. Setup completes only after you explicitly approve that review.

## Normal daily use

After onboarding, speak naturally:

> Find jobs for me in Boston and NYC.

> Which of these are worth applying to?

> Build applications for the top three.

> I want to apply to number four anyway.

> I have an interview with Company X next week.

> I submitted these two today.

The system handles routine research, evidence selection, file creation, reviewer routing, validation, and workflow bookkeeping behind the scenes. You should not have to select internal Skills, edit schemas, assign evidence IDs, or manage YAML.

You still control factual corrections, pursue/skip decisions, legal or work-authorization answers, compensation commitments, final artifact approval, interview and offer decisions, and every external action. **You submit applications and send networking messages yourself.**

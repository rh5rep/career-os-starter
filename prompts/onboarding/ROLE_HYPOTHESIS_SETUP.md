# Role Hypothesis Setup

## Copy/paste prompt

Using my approved evidence and preferences, help me create role-family hypotheses. Keep function, industry/domain, and work environment separate. Propose PRIMARY, ADJACENT, EXPERIMENTAL, DEPRIORITIZED, and EXCLUDED families. For each family record why it may fit, supporting evidence IDs, gaps, market signal if known, my stated interest, outcomes so far if any, and confidence SUPPORTED/SUGGESTIVE/UNKNOWN.

Do not rank me into one permanent identity. Do not downgrade a family because of one rejection. Mark suggestions that come from your inference rather than my stated interest. Ask for my approval before treating any family as PRIMARY or EXCLUDED.

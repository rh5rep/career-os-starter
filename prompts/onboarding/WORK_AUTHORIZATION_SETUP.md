# Work Authorization Setup

## Copy/paste prompt

Help me record work authorization and sponsorship facts country by country. Ask only for facts I know or can document: citizenship/residence basis, permit type, validity dates, employer specificity, work-hour limits, sponsorship needs, pending applications, and official source if available.

Never infer authorization from where I studied, lived, or worked. Do not provide a consequential application answer from incomplete information. Mark uncertain jurisdictions UNKNOWN or HOLD and create a short list of what must be verified. In application forms, label unresolved legal questions `NEEDS_HUMAN_ANSWER`.

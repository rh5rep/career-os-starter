# Work History Interview

## Copy/paste prompt

Interview me about one role at a time. For each role capture: employer/organization, exact title, dates, employment type, team/context, responsibilities, specific projects, problem/objective, my personal contribution, collaborators, tools/methods, outputs, outcomes, metrics, customers/users/stakeholders, artifacts/proof, and anything I am uncertain about.

Do not let vague verbs like “led,” “owned,” “built,” “managed,” “improved,” or “increased” pass without clarifying scope. Ask what I personally did versus what the team did. If a metric is remembered but not verified, mark it HOLD rather than deleting it or presenting it as fact.

After each role, return a compact evidence draft with candidate evidence IDs, status, source/proof, safe claims, and `not_justified` boundaries. Ask me to correct it before moving to the next role.

## Copy/paste prompt

Interview me about job-search preferences and classify each answer as HARD CONSTRAINT, STRONG PREFERENCE, SOFT PREFERENCE, or EXPERIMENTAL HYPOTHESIS. Cover locations, relocation, remote/hybrid/onsite, commute, travel, industries, excluded industries, role types, company size/stage, compensation, work-life preferences, mission, management style, team environment, and lifestyle considerations.

Do not silently turn a preference into a hard filter. For compensation, distinguish an actual floor from a target or preference. For geography, distinguish current residence from target location. Summarize tradeoffs and contradictions for me to resolve before updating `canonical/CAREER_PREFERENCES.md`.

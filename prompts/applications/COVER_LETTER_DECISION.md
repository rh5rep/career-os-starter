# Cover Letter Decision

## Copy/paste prompt

Decide whether a bespoke cover/motivation letter is worth creating for **{{ROLE}}**. Consider whether it is required, high priority, whether company-specific motivation adds real information, and whether the candidate needs a coherent bridge that the resume/CV cannot carry.

If creating one, use verified company intelligence plus confirmed human resonance and 2–3 selected evidence items. Build a coherent story rather than restating the CV. If there is no honest company/personal hook, do not manufacture one. Return CREATE, DO_NOT_CREATE, DEFER, or HOLD with a concise reason.

# Outcome Closeout

## Copy/paste prompt

Record the confirmed outcome for **{{ROLE}}** using the most specific process event supported by the source: rejection, withdrawal, role closed, offer received/accepted/declined, or another explicit event. Preserve employer wording when useful. Update workflow state separately from process stage.

Then identify possible learning observations without asserting causality. One outcome may be evidence that something happened; it is not proof why it happened. Any material workflow change should become a learning proposal, not an automatic rule rewrite.

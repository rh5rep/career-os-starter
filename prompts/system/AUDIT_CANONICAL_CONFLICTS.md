# Audit Canonical Conflicts

## Copy/paste prompt

Audit the canonical files and any sources I provide for conflicts in identity, dates, titles, education, metrics, ownership, skills/proficiency, authorization, preferences, and outcomes. Follow the authority order. Return conflicts with exact sources and the decision needed; do not reconcile by plausibility or majority vote.

Also flag generated/historical artifacts that contain stale values, but do not treat them as factual authority or rewrite history automatically.

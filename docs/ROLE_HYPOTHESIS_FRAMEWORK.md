# Role-Hypothesis Framework

A role family is a testable career hypothesis, not a permanent identity.

## Lanes

- **PRIMARY** — currently one of the main search lanes.
- **ADJACENT** — plausible transfer of existing evidence into a neighboring function.
- **EXPERIMENTAL** — deliberately testing a less-established path.
- **DEPRIORITIZED** — not worth much current search effort, but not forbidden.
- **EXCLUDED** — intentionally out of scope, normally because of a hard constraint or clear human choice.

## Record for every family

Store: why it may fit, supporting evidence IDs, gaps, market signal, candidate interest, outcomes so far, and confidence (`SUPPORTED`, `SUGGESTIVE`, `UNKNOWN`). Keep function, industry/domain, and environment separate. “Healthcare” is a domain; “operations” is a function; “early-stage company” is an environment.

## Updating hypotheses

Use market discovery, networking, applications, screens, interviews, and outcomes. A single rejection does not automatically downgrade a family. A single interview does not prove it is the best family. Proposals should state what changed and how much evidence supports the change.

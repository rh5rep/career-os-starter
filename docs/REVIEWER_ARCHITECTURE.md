# Reviewer Architecture

Independent review exists because the drafting pass should not self-approve.

## Universal lenses

**Recruiter:** fast comprehension, level/location clarity, obvious relevance, scan quality, and trust risks.

**Hiring Manager:** whether the evidence actually supports the work described, whether finite space is well allocated, and what gaps remain.

## Configurable third lens

The third reviewer is derived from the exact JD and may be a senior marketing leader, FP&A manager, operations leader, engagement manager, implementation/customer-success leader, design lead, researcher, educator, engineer, policy specialist, or another domain-relevant expert.

The reviewer is a **lens**, not a simulation of an actual employee. It must not invent private hiring criteria, motives, or likely employer decisions.

## Isolation

Each reviewer starts from the same verified inputs and does not see generator rationale or other conclusions before its own pass. Afterward, findings are consolidated while preserving attribution and disagreement.

## Findings and dispositions

Every material finding gets one disposition: `FIXED`, `ACCEPTED_RISK`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `REJECTED_FINDING`, or `UNRESOLVED`. Reviewer agreement is not career evidence.

## v0.1 pilot note

Three lenses for every serious application are retained as a **pilot default**, not a proven permanent rule. Track unique findings, duplicates, material changes, human overrides, and effort. A future learning proposal may reduce or change the reviewer set if evidence supports it.

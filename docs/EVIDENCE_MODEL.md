# Evidence Model

## Plain-English idea

The Career OS separates **what happened** from **how we describe it**. A resume sentence is not proof. The evidence ledger is the place where a candidate records the underlying experience, source, uncertainty, and the boundaries on what may safely be claimed.

## Status vocabulary

- **VERIFIED** — directly confirmed by a reliable source or the candidate with enough specificity to use as fact.
- **SUPPORTED** — credible and usable, but the support is weaker, indirect, or less precise than VERIFIED.
- **HOLD** — potentially useful, but do not use externally until a named uncertainty is resolved.
- **UNKNOWN** — not established. Do not guess.

These statuses describe evidence confidence, not how impressive something is.

## What an evidence record should distinguish

A strong record separates: context/fact, interpretation, objective/problem, personal contribution, team contribution, ownership, tools/methods, proficiency, outputs, outcomes, metrics, collaborators/stakeholders, source/proof, uncertainty, and `not_justified` overstatement boundaries.

### Why contribution and ownership are separate

A person can make a material contribution without owning the whole strategy, product, account, budget, project, or architecture. The system should preserve that distinction instead of upgrading strong participation into leadership language.

### Why outcomes and metrics are separate

A team outcome can be real while an individual should not claim personal ownership of the entire result. Metrics also need scope: denominator, time period, attribution, and whether they were measured or remembered.

## Non-engineering examples

**Marketing**
- VERIFIED: “Built weekly newsletter campaigns in Mailchimp.”
- Not automatically justified: “Owned lifecycle marketing strategy.”
- Clarifying evidence needed for the broader claim: who chose audience/segmentation, channel strategy, cadence, experimentation, KPI targets, and cross-channel lifecycle design?

**Operations**
- VERIFIED: “Maintained the scheduling spreadsheet used by 15 staff.”
- Not automatically justified: “Led workforce operations.”
- Clarifying evidence needed: staffing decisions, policy ownership, forecasting, people management, and operating authority.

**Consulting**
- VERIFIED: “Prepared client analysis and presented findings.”
- Not automatically justified: “Owned the client relationship.”
- Clarifying evidence needed: commercial responsibility, stakeholder ownership, scope negotiation, account management, and decision authority.

**Customer Success**
- VERIFIED: “Ran onboarding calls and documented customer issues.”
- Not automatically justified: “Owned renewals and retention strategy.”

**Finance**
- VERIFIED: “Updated monthly forecast models and reconciled variance inputs.”
- Not automatically justified: “Owned FP&A forecasting.”

**Research**
- VERIFIED: “Collected data, ran the specified analysis, and co-authored a poster.”
- Not automatically justified: “Designed and led the study.”

## External-claim gate

Applicant-facing claims may use VERIFIED or appropriately scoped SUPPORTED evidence. HOLD and UNKNOWN may be discussed internally as gaps, but must not become facts in a resume, application answer, letter, portfolio, or interview script unless resolved.

## Source hierarchy

Use the repository authority order. When a higher-authority source conflicts with a lower one, surface the conflict. Do not pick the version that sounds better.

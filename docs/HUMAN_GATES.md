# Human Gates

The Career OS automates preparation, analysis, organization, and quality control while preserving human agency at consequential moments.

## Required human approval

| Gate | AI may do | Human owns |
|---|---|---|
| Pursue / skip | Verify role, map evidence/gaps, present tradeoffs | Decide whether to invest/apply |
| Ambiguous claim/positioning | Surface options and boundaries | Approve material interpretation |
| Work authorization / legal attestation | Retrieve canonical facts, identify missing information | Confirm consequential answer |
| Compensation commitment | Research context and prepare ranges/scenarios | Approve submitted number/commitment |
| Artifact release | Draft, review, render, QA | Approve document sent under candidate's name |
| Application submission | Prepare form/materials and preflight | Submit/authorize submission |
| Networking | Research contact, draft message, suggest timing | Decide relationship value and send |
| Interview commitment | Prepare scheduling/options | Accept/commit |
| Offer | Analyze terms/tradeoffs | Accept, decline, or negotiate commitments |
| Workflow rule change | Draft evidence-backed proposal | Approve material change |

## No unnecessary gates

Do not interrupt the candidate for trivial internal bookkeeping. Automate stable deterministic steps such as validation, deduplication, file checks, and formatting where safe. Human attention is reserved for choices where values, legal facts, identity, relationships, or external commitments matter.

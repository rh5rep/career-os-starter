# Role Hypotheses

Role families are hypotheses to test against market evidence, candidate interest, applications, networking, interviews, and outcomes. They are not permanent identity labels.

Allowed lanes:
- `PRIMARY`
- `ADJACENT`
- `EXPERIMENTAL`
- `DEPRIORITIZED`
- `EXCLUDED`

Allowed confidence:
- `SUPPORTED`
- `SUGGESTIVE`
- `UNKNOWN`

## Rules
- One rejection does not downgrade a role family.
- One interview does not prove a role family is “best.”
- Keep function, industry/domain, and work environment separate when possible.
- A role can be attractive but currently weakly supported; record both facts instead of forcing a single score.

# 4 — Record Submission / Process Update

Use natural language, for example:

> I submitted Company A and Company B today.

or:

> Company C invited me to a recruiter screen next Thursday.

or:

> Company D rejected me today.

The system should record only what you actually confirmed, preserve exact submitted materials when known, update process stage separately from workflow state, and tell you the next action or review trigger. Generated files or plans never count as submission by themselves.
